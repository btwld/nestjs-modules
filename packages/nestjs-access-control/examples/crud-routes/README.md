# CRUD Routes

An invoices API generated by `@concepta/nestjs-crud`, used by two roles:

| Role | May |
| --- | --- |
| `clerk` | create, read and update invoices, never seeing their `notes`; not change an `amount` once entered |
| `manager` | create, read, update and delete invoices |

The routes are built in [`invoice.crud.ts`](./invoice.crud.ts): each
operation declares the grant it needs through `extraDecorators`.
[`crud-routes.module.ts`](./crud-routes.module.ts) registers
`AccessControlCrudPolicy` as `CrudModule`'s request policy.

## What to look at

- **Query input is checked too.** A clerk's list leaves out `notes`, and a
  `filter`, `or`, `sort` or `s` (search) naming it is refused: ordering or
  matching by a field tells its value without showing it. The manager may.
  `select` is left to the response filter and the repository.
- **Write fields on generated routes.** No handler of yours runs, so the
  request policy checks the body: the clerk's change to `amount` is
  refused, their change to `customer` goes through.
- **Batches are checked item by item.** A clerk's `POST /invoices/bulk`
  checks every item's fields, and what comes back leaves out `notes` on
  every row.
- **Replace writes everything.** A `PUT` erases what it leaves out, so a
  clerk, who may not write `amount`, may not replace an invoice at all.
